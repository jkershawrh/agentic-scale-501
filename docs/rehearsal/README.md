## What remains gated

The following are future live capabilities and are not claimed by this lab:

- `/api/scale/run` live execution;
- runtime human-approval enforcement for mutating actions;
- complete target OpenTelemetry correlation;
- approved Intel Xeon identity, allocation, utilization, and throughput telemetry;
- controlled OpenShift or dependency pressure injection;
- bounded recovery evidence from a target environment;
- approved thresholds;
- immutable publication and Launchpad 1 / 5 / 25-seat certification.

## Source-label rule

Every displayed or quoted metric must carry its source classification. Say “the rehearsal fixture shows” for these files. Do not rewrite that as “OpenShift achieved,” “Intel delivered,” or “the workload supports” a capacity level.

Red Hat OpenShift is the intended governed execution surface. Intel Xeon telemetry is part of the intended resource proof. Neither is observed by this local rehearsal.

## Authority rule

The runner and deterministic checker may assemble evidence and identify violations. They may not promote the workload. Final disposition remains with an identified human reviewer, followed by independent certification when the separate gates are complete.
