# Facilitator guide

## Suggested timing

| Segment | Minutes | Facilitation focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Preflight and declare | 15 | Source labels, prerequisites, immutable identity |
| Baseline and sustain | 20 | Joined proof rather than a throughput-only claim |
| Pressure and recovery | 25 | Boundedness, reversibility, restoration evidence |
| Score and peer review | 20 | Missing proof versus threshold breach |
| Close and handoff | 10 | Narrow claims and human authority |

## Prompting questions

- What could stay “up” while quality or governance silently fails?
- Which field proves this phase belongs to the same comparison?
- Is this metric observed, rehearsed, or proposed?
- What evidence would make this conclusion inconclusive?
- Who can change the disposition, and where is that authority recorded?

## Common corrections

- Replace “the platform handled” with “the rehearsal fixture represents.”
- Replace “passed” with “rehearsal complete” when thresholds are unapproved.
- Reject a comparison if image, evaluation set, target, model, policy, or run correlation changes silently.
- Treat missing Intel identity or target telemetry as absent proof, not as zero utilization.
- Require removal and restoration evidence before calling pressure testing complete.

## Validation rubric

A learner completes the lab when they can:

1. reproduce the local checker result;
2. trace a metric back to an exact proof path and source label;
3. identify the immutable identity and intended changed condition;
4. distinguish incomplete evidence from a threshold breach;
5. close without a live OpenShift, Intel, or certification claim;
6. preserve the human-owned promotion boundary.
