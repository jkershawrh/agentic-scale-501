== Review order

. *Admissibility:* correct source, prerequisite, approved profile, immutable identity.
. *Completeness:* every required field and every correlated journey.
. *Safety:* no unauthorized action; bounded pressure removed.
. *Usefulness:* quality, throughput, latency, errors, and timeouts.
. *Recovery:* restoration and post-recovery quality within approved limits.
. *Authority:* machine recommendation only; human disposition and promotion.

Use the link:{attachmentsdir}/templates/scorecard.md[review scorecard,window=_blank]. Missing evidence is `INCONCLUSIVE`, not zero and not pass. A threshold breach is distinct from missing proof.

=== Review lens

* *Source:* live, rehearsal, and offline evidence remain distinct.
* *Thresholds:* only approved thresholds can produce a pass or fail.
* *Completeness:* missing required evidence is explicitly inconclusive.
* *Safety:* unauthorized action and incomplete restoration fail closed.
* *Reproducibility:* every score cites an exact evidence path.
* *Authority:* the reviewer owns disposition and promotion.
* *Limitations:* unresolved blockers remain visible in the result.
* *Lifecycle:* cleanup and residue evidence are part of the decision.
* *Comparison:* every phase retains the declared identity.
* *Handoff:* the next owner receives evidence rather than an oral claim.

== Learner checkpoint

Choose `REHEARSAL COMPLETE` or `INCONCLUSIVE`, and cite at least three proof paths. Do not select a live certification outcome.

*Pass when:* a reviewer can reproduce your recommendation and see every blocker without trusting prose alone.

== Verify

Trace at least three recommendation statements to exact fields in the proof envelopes. Confirm that missing evidence produces `INCONCLUSIVE`, while an observed threshold breach produces a separate failing result. Do not convert either state into zero or silently omit it.

Check that the source remains rehearsal, the threshold set remains unapproved, Intel telemetry is not represented as observed, and reviewer disposition remains pending. Confirm that automated promotion is false in every phase.

Have another learner reproduce the recommendation from the evidence paths and declaration without using your prose summary. Record disagreements for the human reviewer rather than asking the runtime to resolve authority.

== Key takeaway

Deterministic scoring makes evidence review repeatable, but certification remains a human-owned decision. A defensible recommendation exposes its sources, limits, and unresolved blockers.
