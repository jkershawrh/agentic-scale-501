== Evidence review

Trace these joins rather than reading one headline metric:

. `runId` joins the phase to the declared run.
. `correlation.completeJourneys` accounts for every expected journey.
. `policy` shows evaluated, compliant, and unauthorized activity.
. `quality` identifies the scorer and evaluation count.
. `inference` attributes model, endpoint, latency, and token use.
. `resources` names its telemetry source; a rehearsal hardware label is not approved hardware proof.
. `authority` preserves human review and disables automated promotion.

== Learner checkpoint

Record the baseline p95 journey latency, inference p95, quality score, policy compliance, correlation ratio, and source. Add the sentence: “These are rehearsal fixture values, not observed OpenShift or Intel performance.”

*Pass when:* your baseline note includes source and provenance, not just performance numbers.
