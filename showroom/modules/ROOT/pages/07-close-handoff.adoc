= 7. Package your evidence
:navtitle: 7. Package

== RUN NOW — create the leave-behind

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
node content-501/tools/qualification-runner.mjs package
tar -tzf "$HOME/agentic-501-evidence.tgz"
----

Verify the artifact hashes:

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
cd "$HOME/agentic-501-evidence"
sha256sum -c evidence-manifest.sha256
----

Your package contains the profile, all four phase envelopes, the generated comparison report, your human decision, and a hash manifest. It is portable and reviewable without the facilitator's memory.

== What you proved

You executed a participant-owned workload through baseline, sustained load, bounded pressure, and recovery. You preserved evidence correlation, policy, and human authority and created a reproducible handoff.

== What you did not prove

The package does not prove production capacity, shared model behavior, approved Intel Xeon utilization, multi-seat scale, or Launchpad certification. Those require admitted live sources and independent review.

== Cleanup and reclaim boundary

The workload ran only inside your terminal process and has stopped. The evidence files remain intentionally as your leave-behind. Launchpad still owns namespace, route, credential, and lab reclamation.

== Completion standard

The lab is complete when every manifest entry verifies, the archive exists, and your disposition cites evidence rather than intuition.
