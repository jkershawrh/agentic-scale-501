= Agentic AI 501 — Scale and Certify
:navtitle: Journey overview

== Your mission

A governed agent service is preparing for a larger rollout. Leadership does not want a screenshot showing that the service was healthy once. They need a reviewable answer to a harder question:

*Does the service remain useful, governed, attributable, and recoverable as demand changes?*

You are the qualification engineer. You will execute a bounded workload, increase concurrency, introduce one safe local pressure condition, remove it, and package the resulting evidence for a human release decision.

== Show → Learn → Do → Prove

* *Show:* see the difference between availability and an evidence-backed operating envelope.
* *Learn:* freeze comparison identity, preserve policy and human authority, and fail closed when evidence is missing.
* *Do:* create your own run, execute baseline, sustained, pressure, and recovery phases, and observe how the measurements change.
* *Prove:* leave with a portable evidence package containing your profile, four proof envelopes, report, decision record, and hashes.

== What is real in this lab

The commands execute a deterministic workload in your participant terminal and measure that execution. They create a participant-owned, learner-controlled evidence set under `$HOME/agentic-501-evidence`. The controlled pressure is local and bounded; it does not inject a cluster fault or affect another seat.

IMPORTANT: The evidence source is *OFFLINE*. This lab proves the qualification method and your execution of it. It does not claim production capacity, approved Intel Xeon utilization, or Launchpad certification. Those claims require independently admitted live telemetry and human review.
